# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Financial Timeline is a personal financial monitoring application intended for private use.

The application may connect to financial institutions through Enable Banking in order to retrieve account balance information for balance verification and reconciliation.

Financial data obtained through these connections is used only for the operation of Financial Timeline. It is not sold, shared for advertising purposes, or used for profiling.

Authentication credentials for bank accounts are not collected or stored by Financial Timeline. Bank authentication is handled by the relevant financial institution and Enable Banking.
