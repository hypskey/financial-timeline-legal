# Terms of Use

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Financial Timeline is a personal financial monitoring application provided for private use.

The application may use third-party services, including Enable Banking and participating financial institutions, to retrieve account balance information.

Financial Timeline is intended as an informational and reconciliation tool. It does not provide banking, investment, accounting, tax, or financial advisory services.

Availability and accuracy of externally supplied banking information may depend on the connected financial institution and third-party services.

Use of the application is at the user's own discretion.
